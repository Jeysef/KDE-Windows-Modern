A **Windows 11-style Start menu** for KDE Plasma 6.

- **Pinned:** a grid of your apps; drag to reorder
- **All apps:** alphabetical list with folders
- **Search** as you type, across apps, files, settings and actions; Enter runs the best match, and a mouse pointer resting over the results does not steal the selection
- Places column, your account, and a split Shut down button for the other power options

Add it with *Add Widgets → Windows Modern Start Menu*, then remove Plasma's application launcher.

It is part of [Windows Modern](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern), which also brings the taskbar, system tray with Quick Settings, Snap Layouts, shortcuts and themes. It looks best with them, but works on its own.

Based on Menu11-Next by Eisteed and OnzeMenuKDE by adhec. Requires KDE Plasma 6. License: GPL-3.0-or-later.
