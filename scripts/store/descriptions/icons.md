A **Windows 11-style icon theme** for KDE Plasma 6: colorful folders, apps, places, devices and file types, with symbolic icons for panels and toolbars that follow your light or dark colors.

Curated from Eleven (mjkim0727), with gaps filled from Fluent (vinceliuice) and a few others; see [ATTRIBUTION.md](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern/blob/main/ATTRIBUTION.md).

Choose it in *System Settings → Colors & Themes → Icons*.

Part of [Windows Modern](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern), a Windows 11 look and feel for Plasma 6; the Windows Modern global themes install it automatically. License: GPL-3.0.
