Windows' **Show desktop** corner for the Plasma 6 panel: a thin sliver at the far right. Click it to minimize every window; click again to bring them back.

Add it with *Add Widgets → Windows Modern Show Desktop*, at the right end of the panel.

Part of [Windows Modern](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern), a Windows 11 look and feel for Plasma 6; the Windows Modern global themes install it automatically. License: GPL-3.0.
