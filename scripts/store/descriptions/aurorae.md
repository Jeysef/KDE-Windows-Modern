**Windows-style window decorations** for KDE Plasma 6 ({VARIANT}): wide minimize, maximize and close buttons with Windows' red close hover, and square corners, a blend of Windows 11 and Windows 10. A {OTHER} variant is a separate item.

Choose it in *System Settings → Colors & Themes → Window Decorations*.

Part of [Windows Modern](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern), a Windows 11 look and feel for Plasma 6; the Windows Modern global themes install it automatically. Based on Win11OS-kde by yeyushengfan258. License: GPL-3.0.
