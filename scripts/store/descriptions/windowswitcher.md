A **Windows 11-style Alt+Tab** switcher for KWin. Every preview has the same height and keeps its window's shape, the cards wrap into centered rows, and the selected window gets a rounded focus ring. Hover a card for its close button, click to switch, and move with the arrow keys. It follows your light or dark colors.

Choose it in *System Settings → Window Management → Task Switcher*.

Part of [Windows Modern](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern), a Windows 11 look and feel for Plasma 6; the Windows Modern global themes install it automatically. License: GPL-3.0.
