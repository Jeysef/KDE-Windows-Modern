A **Windows 11-style Plasma theme** for KDE Plasma 6 ({VARIANT}): the panel, popups, tooltips, switches and widgets drawn the way Windows 11 draws its taskbar and flyouts. A {OTHER} variant is a separate item.

Choose it in *System Settings → Colors & Themes → Plasma Style*.

Part of [Windows Modern](https://github.com/Jeysef/KDE-Windows-Modern), a Windows 11 look and feel for Plasma 6; the Windows Modern global themes install it automatically. Based on Win11OS-kde by yeyushengfan258. License: GPL-3.0.
